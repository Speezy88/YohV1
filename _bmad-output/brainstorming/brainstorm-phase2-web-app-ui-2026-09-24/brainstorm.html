direction

direction

direction

direction

technique two

Six Thinking Hats

Five hats were worn (no white hat this round) — each one pointed the same brief at a different light.

🎩

Red Hat

feelings, gut reactions
insight

Never want the feeling that Yoh is "stupid" — the effort invested must pay off as miles better than any other AI product.

insight

Every capability built has to be exhibited at the highest level, right in front of the user — without cluttering the UI.

insight

A "stupid" moment: planning without life context — scheduling homework right after school, ignoring commute, food, transition time.

🎩

Yellow Hat

benefits, optimism
insight

"It gets me" = a valid plan aligned with real work habits — that also nudges toward being more organized.

idea

Recurring daily time — commute, meals, routines — shows up as calendar blocks. Not everything gets blocked.

idea

Those blocks drag to a better time directly, building on FR-27's confirm-gated calendar editing.

🎩

Black Hat

risk, failure modes
failure mode

Data is too hard to input.

failure mode

Plans are too rigid instead of flexible.

failure mode

It breaks easily — reliability.

break

Chat doesn't close out a conversation when it naturally should.

break

Yoh hallucinates its own capabilities — asks for data it was never going to store or use.

break

It produces a terrible plan.

break

Capabilities gated off by missing data — instead it should do as much as it can with what it has. Graceful degradation, not hard gates.

🎩

creative leaps

Layout & design

The neumorphism, the off-white-and-black, the feel of the thing.

One system, not two. /sandbox is the input that makes the reshuffle smart. Per graceful degradation, the reshuffle still has to work — best effort — even when that data is missing.
The dropped idea, resurfacing. Trust turns out to live inside the action, not in a folder: /sandbox pings only after the real Notion write; reshuffle shows a preview before it applies. Receipts, reinvented in place.
Groundwork — an event log. Phase 2 needs a Yoh-owned completion log (tasks leave Notion the moment they're checked). It feeds the completed list, minutes worked, on-time rate, streak, heatmap, and future self-calibration.
Groundwork — a wider confirm path. AD-3 names chat-cli.ts as the only caller of apply(proposal). One-click approve on the web needs that rule generalized to a surface-agnostic confirm path.
Anti-clutter, in one line. One job per page — what/when, talk, find, reflect — plus slash commands instead of buttons. The dot-field screensaver has to be custom-built (the transitions.dev version is Pro-only), and the off-white neumorphism still needs a contrast check.
