# PRD Quality Review — Yoh (YohV1), Epic 13 additions (2026-09-29)

Scope: §5.14 (FR-52..FR-60), the 2026-09-29 amendments (FR-15/FR-17 notes, glossary, §6, §7, §8, §9.3a, §9.4, §10 SM-8/SM-9/SM-C5/SM-C6, §11 items 14-15, §12) and addendum.md "Epic 13: Memory — Technical Notes". Solo-stakes personal tool, chain-top PRD (feeds UX, architecture, stories).

## Overall verdict
The Epic 13 design is coherent and unusually well-scoped for a personal tool. Visible, deletable memory, a receipt with Undo, deterministic commands, propose-then-confirm for rule changes and patterns, and an explicit sensitive-inference ban all hold up. The risk is at the seams. The Epic was added as an overlay, so FR-16, §5.11, FR-42, SM-4 and the glossary were only partly reconciled. Several FRs also leave edge behaviors (undo after supersede, deleting a source chat, full always-loaded set, how a confirmed rule change is stored) undefined, and the privacy sentence contradicts FR-56 as written. Fix the highs before `bmad-ux` and story creation.

## Decision-readiness — adequate
Real decisions are stated as decisions: no embeddings (§9.4, with a revisit trigger at ~10,000 items), Rating replaces Self-Check, the FR-56 "no confirmation for Feedback" ruling, and the sensitive-inference ban. §11 items 14-15 are honest deferrals with owners. Trade-offs are thin, though. "Feedback ... changes Yoh's behavior from the next turn onward, with no confirmation step" (FR-56) trades FR-16's blanket confirm rule for convenience, and the PRD never says so. The cost of an extra Haiku call per non-trivial turn (addendum "Filing") is not weighed against §7 Cost.

### Findings
- **high** FR-16 not reconciled with FR-53/FR-56 (§5.3 FR-16 vs FR-56, FR-53). FR-16 says "Any learned behavioral pattern ... requires Spencer's explicit confirmation before Yoh acts on it." FR-53 allows Yoh-*inferred* items in Feedback, Corrections and About you (always loaded), and FR-56 says Feedback changes behavior "with no confirmation step." Nothing says whether an inferred item can steer behavior, and Yoh-inferred versus Patterns (FR-58) is blurry. *Fix:* amend FR-16 with one line drawing the boundary (stated items take effect directly; inferred items, and any behavior change from a pattern, are gated), and state that inferred items either are not loaded until confirmed or are limited to context and never change behavior.
- **medium** Trade-off not named (FR-56). Say what "no confirmation" gives up (one misheard Feedback item silently shifts behavior) and point to the mitigation (receipt + Undo). *Fix:* one sentence in FR-56.

## Substance over theater — strong
Nothing here is furniture. The research digest in the addendum maps each named failure mode to a specific FR (bloat, stale facts, wrong inferences, creepy recall, over-generalization). No persona or NFR boilerplate.
- **low** Addendum claim "keyword search reaches over 90% recall under about 1k documents" is unsourced and mismatched with the PRD's own scale trigger (§9.4 says hundreds of items, revisit at ~10,000). *Fix:* cite the source or drop the number, and align the scale figures.

## Strategic coherence — adequate
Thesis is clear: "Yoh gets to know Spencer without being told twice, and Yoh corrects its own behavior over time" (§5.14 Description). SM-8 and SM-9 map to it, and SM-C5/SM-C6 counterbalance them properly. Weak points are the leftover Self-Check machinery and metrics that are hard to measure.

### Findings
- **high** Self-Check not fully retired (§5.5 heading "Memory, Learning & Self-Check"; §5.13 FR-19; §10 SM-4; §7 glossary "Escalate-Under-Strain"; §9 in-scope line "periodic Self-Check (FR-17)"). SM-4 still says "Self-Check scores ... Validates FR-17, FR-19"; SM-C2 still measures "Self-Check prompt frequency"; the Escalate-Under-Strain glossary entry still lists "Self-Check frequency." Only SM-1 was amended. Metrics now validate a retired requirement. *Fix:* rewrite SM-4/SM-C2 against Rating (FR-60), update the glossary entry and the §9 line, and rename §5.5.
- **medium** SM-9 "feedback given after a 1 is not given again for the same behavior" cannot be measured without a judgment call about "same behavior," and with at most one Rating per day the monthly trend has few data points. *Fix:* define the observable (e.g. count of Feedback items superseded or restated within 30 days of a 1) and note the small-n caveat.
- **medium** SM-8 measured by "how often he restates an existing Memory Item" relies on FR-54's restate-detection, but a restate can be a legitimate refinement. *Fix:* say which counter (UPDATE-by-restatement events) is the metric.

## Done-ness clarity — thin
Most FRs have a Consequences list with good testable lines (FR-54 failure path, FR-55 ambiguous-forget, FR-57 worked example). But several load-bearing rules are adjectives, and key edge cases are unspecified.

### Findings
- **high** Undo and Forget semantics after supersede/update (FR-54, FR-55, FR-59). "Undo removes the item completely" — but if the item superseded an older one, or updated one, is the older one restored? Does "forget that" on the newer item revive the older? Are superseded versions purged when their current item is forgotten ("Every Memory Item is visible and deletable," §8)? Is there an Undo time limit, or does it survive a reload? "forget ..." with no match is unspecified. *Fix:* state (a) Undo of a supersede or update restores the prior version, (b) delete removes the item and its history, (c) the Undo window (or point to FR-41's), (d) no-match reply.
- **high** FR-56 cap behavior contradicts itself. "Every model call ... includes the always-loaded folders" versus "a fixed size cap ... Yoh files the item, flags the folder as full ... Nothing is dropped silently." If the set exceeds the cap, is the new item loaded? Which items are included? Testable outcome missing. *Fix:* specify that over-cap items are stored but not loaded (or that the oldest/lowest-value are excluded), and that the review flag shows exactly which are excluded.
- **high** FR-57 leaves the mechanism for "changing a built-in rule" undefined. Work-start times, protected windows, the Time Budget, and Derived Priority weights are today code/constants (Time Budget is a per-day declaration, FR-5, not a rule). After a yes, where does the changed value live, can Spencer revert it, and is the "declined" preference loaded (it is in an always-loaded folder) while the Proposal is pending or after a no? Detecting "conflicts with a built-in rule" is an LLM judgment with no listed rules ("such as" is open-ended). *Fix:* enumerate the overridable rules, state that a confirmed change is stored as an explicit setting visible on the Memory page with revert, and state that a pending or declined preference does not change planning.
- **medium** FR-60 cadence is untestable and internally tense: "Now and then" versus "At most one prompt per day, at a randomized turn," and "A 1 brings the next prompt forward: it may come on the next substantive turn." Does the pull-forward override the one-per-day cap the same day? What counts as dismissed and does dismissal count toward the day? *Fix:* state the cap precisely (e.g. one per calendar day in `YOH_TIMEZONE`, the 1-pull-forward applies from the next day or next substantive turn after a stated minimum gap).
- **medium** FR-53 expiry: "Time-bound items ... carry an expiry" does not say who sets it (Yoh at filing? Spencer?), and "Needs review" has no defined actions (renew / delete / keep as history). "Loaded ... When relevant" and "Only when asked" (Ideas & notes) have no testable definition of "relevant" or "asked." *Fix:* define Yoh proposes expiry at filing and shows it in the receipt; list the review actions; define "asked" as an explicit "what do you remember about ..." / Memory-page query, and "relevant" as keyword match to the request (architecture picks method).
- **medium** Source-turn link versus history deletion (FR-52, FR-53, FR-59). FR-52 lets Spencer delete a conversation or clear all history while memories survive; FR-59 shows "a link to the chat turn it came from." The link dangles. *Fix:* say the item then shows "source chat deleted."
- **medium** "Clear all history" (FR-52) is irreversible with no confirmation or undo stated, while §6 lists Memory-page deletes as direct-writes. *Fix:* require a confirm step for bulk clear (deleting one item stays direct).
- **medium** Edit semantics (FR-59): does editing an inferred item change it to stated? Can Spencer move an item between folders? Does an edit that restates another item trigger the FR-54 dedupe? *Fix:* one sentence each.
- **low** "Yoh uses a memory only when it changes the answer" (FR-56) is not verifiable as written. *Fix:* keep as a design principle, and add a checkable proxy (no memory content in a reply unless it was in the request's matched items).
- **low** FR-58 "several occurrences over at least two weeks" is a tagged ASSUMPTION, so acceptable, but "several" needs a number by architecture time (already in §11 item 14).
- **low** FR-54: "Trivial turns are skipped without an extra model call where a deterministic check can tell" leaks an implementation mechanism into the FR. Also the sensitive-inference ban ("health, emotions, or finances") has no described enforcement path; the addendum extraction call does not mention it. *Fix:* keep the ban in the FR, and add the classification step to the addendum "Filing" bullet.

## Scope honesty — adequate
§8 gains two crisp memory non-goals, §9.4 carries the embeddings deferral with a numeric trigger, and inline `[ASSUMPTION]` tags round-trip with §12. Open-items density is proportionate.

### Findings
- **high** Privacy statement contradicts FR-56 (§7 Privacy vs FR-56). §7: "Memory text reaches the LLM provider only inside the model calls that already carry chat content." FR-56: "every Plan build" includes the always-loaded folders, and Plan builds and the Morning Ritual run without a chat turn. Also unaddressed: whether memory text may be sent with `/research` or web-search queries to Perplexity. *Fix:* reword §7 to "only in model calls to the LLM provider (chat, capture, Plan build)," and add a Consequence stating memory is never included in web-search or public-feed queries.
- **medium** Missing failure paths: Memory page or search unavailable, Memory store unwritable at startup, the "What was off?" answer containing a sensitive inference (FR-60 files to Feedback "under FR-54's rules," which is fine, but say so explicitly), and backups retaining items Spencer has deleted (§6 says memory is included in backups; §8 says "visible and deletable"). *Fix:* one line in §6 stating backups retain deleted items until they rotate out.
- **low** Cost: the per-turn extraction call is not reflected in §7 Cost. One line, "at most one Haiku call per non-trivial turn."

## Downstream usability — adequate
IDs are contiguous (FR-52–60, SM-8/9, SM-C5/6, §11 items 14-15) and cross-references resolve. Glossary entries for Memory, Memory Item, Memory Folder, Remembered Receipt and Rating exist. Gaps are in cross-section consistency, which will mislead `bmad-ux` most.

### Findings
- **high** §5.11 page order and Web App definition not amended (§5.11 Description "four pages ... in order"; §7 glossary "Web App ... four pages"; §5.11 line "Every page is reachable ... vertical stack"; §11 resolved item "page order is Home, Tasks, Desk, Research Hub"). FR-59 adds a "fifth page, Memory, after Research Hub" and says it "follows ... the page order in §5.11", but §5.11 still lists four and a nav sidebar list of four. A UX reader has two answers. *Fix:* amend §5.11's description, the glossary Web App entry and the sidebar/nav list to five pages, and mark them amended 2026-09-29.
- **high** FR-42 (Chat panel, five slash commands) not reconciled with FR-55 memory commands (§5.11 FR-42 vs FR-55). FR-42 says Chat "supports five slash commands" and the palette "lists every available slash command." FR-55 adds "remember ...", "forget ...", "what do you remember about ..." as plain-word deterministic commands with no slash form, so the Command Palette cannot list them and discovery is undefined. FR-9's amended text does mention FR-60 under FR-42 but nothing mentions FR-55. Where the Rating prompt and the Remembered Receipt render in the Chat panel is deferred to UX (§11 item 14), but FR-42's "closes out a conversation ... rather than prompting for more" is in tension with a Rating prompt appended to a turn. *Fix:* add a FR-42 note that the three memory phrases are plain-word commands, whether `/remember` `/forget` aliases exist and appear in the palette, and that the Rating prompt is an allowed exception to closing out.
- **medium** Glossary gaps: "Conversation" (FR-52 "delete one conversation" has no unit definition), "stated / inferred," "always-loaded," "superseded," "Needs review," "ratings log," and "Chat History" are used as terms of art without entries. FR-53 and elsewhere use lowercase "memory item"/"memory" while the glossary defines "Memory Item"/"Memory" (drift; low). The "Hot Memory"/"Cold Memory" glossary terms are still worded as Yoh's own planning history and could be confused with Memory; the FR-15 note handles this, but the glossary does not.
- **low** FR-59 "follows ... the Accessibility NFR (§6)" is fine, but nothing states Memory page keyboard behavior for delete/edit. UX territory.

## Shape fit — strong
Capability-spec shape for a single-operator tool is right. No UJ is added for Epic 13; for solo stakes that is proportionate, though one short path (say "Spencer tells Yoh to forget X, Undo, then rates") would help `bmad-ux` sequence the receipt/Undo/Rating interactions. Rigor is not over-formalized.
